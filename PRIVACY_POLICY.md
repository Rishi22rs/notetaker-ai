# UNYON Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 15 October 2026  
**Privacy contact:** underratedgames22@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how UNYON collects, uses, stores, and shares personal data when you use our desktop application, website, and related services (the “Service”). UNYON is based in India. “We,” “us,” and “our” refer to the team providing UNYON.

## 1. Data we process

Depending on the features you use, we process:

- **Account information:** your Google account identifier, name, email address, profile image where provided, and authentication and session records.
- **Audio and transcripts:** microphone and computer audio you choose to capture, the resulting transcripts, and speaker labels used to distinguish audio sources.
- **Prompts and reference content:** questions, instructions, conversation context, and text or other content you provide for AI assistance, including relevant excerpts from documents.
- **Session information:** company names, additional context, session dates and status, uploaded resumes and their extracted text, original resume files where uploaded for session history, and usage duration.
- **Payment and usage records:** purchased packs, order and payment identifiers, transaction amounts and status, credit balances, usage deductions, and coupon redemptions. Payments are handled through the payment provider’s checkout; our payment integration uses transaction references rather than collecting full card details.
- **Support and feedback:** messages, feedback, contact details, and attachments you send us.
- **Technical records:** request identifiers, service errors, model names, request sizes, timing, and usage metrics generated to operate and troubleshoot the Service. Our hosting infrastructure may also process connection information such as IP addresses.

Providing microphone or system-audio access allows the app to capture the selected audio sources. Only enable sources you intend to process. Obtain all required permissions from other participants before capturing or sharing their speech or personal data.

## 2. Why we use data

We process data to create and authenticate accounts; transcribe speech; generate AI responses using your questions and context; save and retrieve session history and resumes; process purchases and meter usage; respond to support and deletion requests; diagnose errors; prevent fraud and misuse; protect the Service; and meet legal obligations.

Where consent is required, we seek it through the relevant notice or permission flow. These terms do not replace any separate consent required for a particular processing activity. We may also process information where another basis is permitted or required by applicable law.

## 3. Cloud AI and transcription

UNYON’s AI and transcription features use cloud services and require internet access. They do not run local AI or transcription models on your device.

Relevant audio is transmitted to Groq for transcription. Prompts, transcripts, conversation context, and selected reference excerpts are transmitted to Google’s Gemini service to generate responses. Requests may pass through UNYON’s backend before reaching the provider. Content does not remain exclusively on your device when you use these features.

Provider processing, retention, safety review, and any use for service improvement depend on the provider’s terms and the account plan and settings used. In particular, Google’s Gemini API terms distinguish paid and unpaid services; unpaid-service content may be used to improve Google’s products and may be reviewed by people. Do not submit confidential or sensitive information unless the relevant processing arrangement is appropriate for that information.

Read [Google’s Gemini API terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms) and [Groq’s privacy information](https://groq.com/privacy-policy). We do not promise that third-party providers retain no data or never use content for model improvement.

## 4. Who receives data

We share relevant data with service providers needed to operate UNYON, including Google for sign-in and AI responses, Groq for transcription, Razorpay for payment processing, and hosting and database providers for account and session storage. Each receives data relevant to its role. Their own privacy notices and contractual terms may also apply.

We may disclose data where legally required, to respond to valid legal requests, or where reasonably necessary to investigate fraud and protect users and the Service. If the business is transferred or reorganised, relevant data may be transferred subject to applicable privacy requirements and notice where required.

These disclosures support operation of the Service. They do not grant UNYON permission to use your content for advertising or model training; any additional use requires an appropriate lawful basis and notice or consent.

## 5. Storage and retention

Account data, session history, uploaded resumes, and other content saved on UNYON’s servers remain with us until you email us to request deletion, unless earlier deletion is required by applicable law or the data is no longer needed for its permitted purpose. We do not apply an automatic expiry to purchased usage minutes.

Authentication tokens and temporary records may expire automatically for security or operational reasons. Processing audio or prompts to deliver a response does not mean that every raw audio segment or request is permanently saved as session history. Cloud providers apply their own retention arrangements.

Some notes, settings, cached information, and other app content may be stored on your device. Deleting server data does not automatically erase copies on your device or files you exported. Uninstalling UNYON does not itself delete your server-side account or session records.

## 6. Deletion requests and your choices

To request deletion of your account and server-stored personal data, email **underratedgames22@gmail.com** from your registered email address. State that you want your account and associated data deleted. We may request proportionate information to verify that you control the account; do not send passwords or payment-card details.

We will process verified requests within the time required by applicable law. Records that must be kept for legal, tax, payment-dispute, or fraud-prevention obligations may be retained only to the extent and for the period permitted or required by law. Where backup copies exist, deletion may follow backup rotation; retained copies remain subject to access restrictions and applicable retention obligations.

You can stop audio capture using the app controls and revoke microphone or system-audio permissions in your operating system. Revoking permissions stops future access but does not delete previously processed data. You may stop using cloud features or the Service at any time.

## 7. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to information about processing, access to your data, correction, erasure, withdrawal of consent, grievance redressal, and nomination of a person to exercise rights in circumstances provided by law. Email our privacy contact to make a request or raise a concern.

Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already lawfully carried out. Some features may become unavailable if you decline or withdraw permission for data processing they require. Nothing in this Policy limits rights or complaint routes available under applicable law.

## 8. Security and international processing

We use safeguards designed to protect personal data, including authenticated access and hashed server-side session tokens. No system can guarantee absolute security. Protect your account and device and contact us promptly if you suspect unauthorised access.

Our service providers may process data outside India. Processing locations depend on the provider and infrastructure used. Any international processing remains subject to applicable legal requirements.

## 9. Website and device storage

The app uses device storage for features such as preferences and locally saved content. Our website’s hosting and external resources, such as fonts, may receive technical connection information when your browser requests them. Browser and operating-system settings can restrict some storage or permissions, although doing so may affect functionality.

## 10. Children

UNYON is intended for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly seek personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

## 11. Changes and contact

We may update this Policy as the Service changes. We will publish the revised version with its effective date and provide additional notice or obtain fresh consent where required by law.

For privacy questions, corrections, deletion requests, or complaints, contact **UNYON** at **underratedgames22@gmail.com**.
